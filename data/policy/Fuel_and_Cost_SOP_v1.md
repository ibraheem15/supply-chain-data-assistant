# Southern California Logistics Operations
## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Fuel & Shipping Cost
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** Jan 2021 | **Confidentiality:** Internal Operations Only
*Sample policy for demonstration. Thresholds are illustrative.*

### 1. Fuel Consumption
Fuel use is recorded in litres per hour (`FUEL_RT_LPH`).
* **Maintenance Inspection:** If `FUEL_RT_LPH` exceeds **12 litres per hour**, schedule a vehicle inspection (engine, tyres and load).
* **Withdrawal:** If `FUEL_RT_LPH` exceeds **16 litres per hour**, withdraw the vehicle from service until the inspection is complete.

### 2. Shipping Cost Approval
Shipping cost is recorded in US dollars (`SHIP_COST_USD`).
* **Manager Approval:** A shipment with `SHIP_COST_USD` above **750 USD** requires approval from the Fleet Manager.
* **Cost Review:** If more than a quarter of one lane's shipments exceed 750 USD in a month, the Finance team reviews the lane.

### 3. Reporting
Fuel and cost comparisons must name the period and the grouping used. A saving may only be reported when the figures for both periods are shown.
